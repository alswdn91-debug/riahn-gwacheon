Home/FAQ
How do I book?
Book on Naver, call 02-3418-7111, or send an Instagram DM. You can choose your designer when booking.
Is there parking?
Public parking is available. Please call the salon before your visit for the nearest public lot and whether parking support is offered.
What are your opening hours?
Open every day except Korean holidays (Seollal/Chuseok day). Weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00.
Are the listed prices final?
Prices shown are base prices for S-length hair. Below the collarbone adds ₩20,000 and below the chest adds ₩40,000. Additional charges apply depending on the designer level and products used.
What happens on my first visit?
We check your hair and hold a 1:1 consultation on face shape, head shape, hair texture and styling habits, then suggest a style before the service.
Which designer should I choose?
Owner Minju: hush, high layered and sleek cuts, hush layered perms. Senior team leader Soyu: build, C-curl, layered and grace perms, men's iron perms. Head designer Yerin: layered cuts, medium-length styles, volume straightening, air-wrap perms, men's perms and down perms. Designer Garam: leaf and layered cuts, glossy volume straightening, jelly perms, Angel Ring treatment. Designer Yeonhee: bob cuts, madam cuts, volume perms, root perms and layered perms.
Do you do men's cuts and perms?
Yes. Men's cut ₩25,000, down perm ₩30,000, men's basic perm ₩110,000 and men's color including cut ₩120,000. Men's iron perms are also available.
Do you cut children's hair?
A junior cut is ₩22,000. A bangs trim is ₩5,000.
Can I get root color only?
Yes. Root color A ₩50,000, B ₩70,000, C ₩100,000 and root bleach ₩70,000 are separate menus.
What hair treatments do you offer?
Special ₩70,000, Premium ₩100,000 and Prestige ₩150,000 treatments, plus Angel Ring treatment.
