Perms & Straightening

Perms chosen
after checking your hair

The right curl depends on hair texture, condition and styling habits. We check your hair and consult 1:1 before choosing the perm type and curl size.

Senior team leader Soyu specializes in build, C-curl, layered and grace perms and men's iron perms; owner Minju in hush layered perms; designer Garam in glossy volume straightening and jelly perms; head designer Yerin in volume straightening, air-wrap perms, men's perms and down perms; designer Yeonhee in volume perms, root perms and layered perms.

Recommended for

  • Guests who want a natural build, C-curl or layered perm
  • Anyone wanting frizzy or curly hair smoothed with volume straightening
  • Men looking for a down perm or iron perm
  • Hair with repeated perms or color that needs a consultation first

FAQ

FAQ

Does the price change for long hair?
Prices shown are base prices for S-length hair. Below the collarbone adds ₩20,000 and below the chest adds ₩40,000. Additional charges apply depending on the designer level and products used.
Do you do men's perms?
Yes. Down perm ₩30,000 and men's basic perm ₩110,000. Senior team leader Soyu specializes in men's iron perms, and head designer Yerin in men's perms and down perms.
How much is volume straightening?
Volume straightening starts from ₩200,000 and straightening + setting perm from ₩250,000 (S length). Designer Garam specializes in glossy volume straightening, and head designer Yerin in volume straightening.
