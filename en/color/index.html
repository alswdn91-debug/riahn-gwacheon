Home/Hair Color

Color only the regrowth,
as much as you need

Root color (A·B·C) and root bleach are separate menus, so you can color just the regrowth.

Full color comes in Special, Premium and Prestige levels, and men's color includes a cut. We confirm the tone you want and your hair condition in a 1:1 consultation.

Recommended for

  • Guests who want to touch up the roots only
  • Anyone who needs to re-lighten the roots after bleaching
  • Men who want a cut and color in one visit
  • Anyone who wants advice on a new tone
