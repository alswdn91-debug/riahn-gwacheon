About

A salon that brings out your own mood,
RIAHN Hair Government Complex Gwacheon

RIAHN Hair Government Complex Gwacheon opened in 2024 at 401, 4F, 10-19 Saesulmak-gil, Gwacheon-si, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea, near Government Complex Gwacheon Station.

With 10 styling chairs, five designers including owner Ji Minju offer design cuts (layered, high layered, hush, leaf and sleek cuts), women's perms (hush layered, build, C-curl, grace, jelly and air-wrap perms), glossy volume straightening, men's iron perms and down perms, root color and full color.

Before every service we check your hair and hold a 1:1 consultation on face shape, head shape, hair texture and daily styling habits. Open every day except Korean holidays (Seollal/Chuseok day): weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00. Public parking is available.

Why guests choose us

  • A hair check and 1:1 consultation before every service, matched to your face shape, head shape, texture and styling habits.
  • Five designers, including two RIAHN Top Stylist award winners, each focused on their own specialty.
  • Angel Ring treatment plus three treatment levels: Special, Premium and Prestige.
  • Root color (A·B·C) and root bleach offered as separate menus.
